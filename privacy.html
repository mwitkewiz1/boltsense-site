Bolt Sense — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 September 2026

Bolt Sense is a puzzle game for iPhone, made by an independent solo developer. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version is that it asks for nothing about you personally, and the only thing it sends anywhere is technical crash and error information used to fix bugs.

Bolt Sense does not ask for, collect, or store your personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-in, and no advertising. The app does send crash and error diagnostics to Unity, the company that makes the game engine Bolt Sense is built with, so that crashes and bugs can be found and fixed. That is the only information that leaves your device, and it is not used to identify or track you.

Crash and error diagnostics

Bolt Sense uses Unity Cloud Diagnostics, a crash-reporting service provided by Unity Technologies. When the app crashes or hits an error, it sends a report so the problem can be found and fixed. Those reports contain:

  • crash reports and error logs from the app;
  • basic technical information about the device and how the app was performing at the time, such as the model and operating system version;
  • an anonymous installation identifier, so that repeated reports coming from the same installation can be grouped together.

That identifier is generated for the app installation. It is not your name, your email address, or your Apple ID, it is not linked to your identity, and it is not used to track you across other apps or websites. These reports are used for one purpose — finding and fixing faults in the game. They are not sold, and they are not shared for advertising. Unity's handling of this information is governed by Unity's Privacy Policy.

What the app does not do

  • It does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or any other personal detail.
  • It does not create an account or require you to sign in.
  • It does not use advertising, advertising identifiers, or cross-app tracking.
  • It does not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, location, or health data.
  • It contains no third-party advertising software. Apart from the crash-reporting service described above, it contains no third-party data-collection software.
  • It does not sell your information, and it does not share it for advertising or marketing.

Information stored on your device

The app saves a small amount of information locally on your iPhone so that it works the way you would expect between sessions — for example your sound and haptics settings, and your progress through a puzzle. This information stays on your device. It is not transmitted anywhere, the developer cannot see it, and deleting the app removes it.

Information Apple may handle

Bolt Sense is distributed through Apple. If you install it through the App Store or through TestFlight, Apple may collect information about that installation under Apple's own policies — for example, aggregated download or crash statistics, or, in the case of TestFlight, the email address you used to join the beta. That relationship is between you and Apple. Any such information is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy, not by this one, and the developer does not receive personal information about you from it.

Children

Bolt Sense does not ask any user for personal information, and it does not ask for an age. No profile of any user is created, and the crash diagnostics described above are technical information about the app and the device rather than information about the person using it.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Bolt Sense ever adds a feature that involves your information, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top of this page will change. Material changes will be described here rather than made quietly.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or about the app, can be sent to BoltSenseDev@gmail.com.
