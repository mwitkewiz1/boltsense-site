Bolt Sense — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 August 2026

Bolt Sense is a puzzle game for iPhone, made by an independent solo developer. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version is that it does not take any.

Bolt Sense does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal information. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, and no advertising, and it does not send anything from your device to the developer or to anyone else.

What the app does not do

  • It does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or any other personal detail.
  • It does not create an account or require you to sign in.
  • It does not use analytics, tracking, advertising identifiers, or crash-reporting services.
  • It does not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, location, or health data.
  • It contains no third-party advertising or third-party data-collection software.
  • It does not sell or share information with anyone, because it does not have any to share.

Information stored on your device

The app saves a small amount of information locally on your iPhone so that it works the way you would expect between sessions — for example your sound and haptics settings, and your progress through a puzzle. This information stays on your device. It is not transmitted anywhere, the developer cannot see it, and deleting the app removes it.

Information Apple may handle

Bolt Sense is distributed through Apple. If you install it through the App Store or through TestFlight, Apple may collect information about that installation under Apple's own policies — for example, aggregated download or crash statistics, or, in the case of TestFlight, the email address you used to join the beta. That relationship is between you and Apple. Any such information is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy, not by this one, and the developer does not receive personal information about you from it.

Children

Because Bolt Sense does not collect information from anyone, it does not collect information from children either. No age is requested and no profile of any user is created.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Bolt Sense ever adds a feature that involves your information, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top of this page will change. Material changes will be described here rather than made quietly.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or about the app, can be sent to BoltSenseDev@gmail.com.
